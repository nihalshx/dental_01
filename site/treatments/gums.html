Gum disease treatment

Periodontal care for gums and the bone beneath.

Book a visit Call +974 3102 6780

Periodontics is the dental specialty that focuses on the inflammatory disease that destroys the gums and the other structures supporting your teeth. Caught early, gum disease can be treated and its damage stopped.

At Kings Dental Center

We measure the health of your gums, explain what we find and clean beneath the gum line where your toothbrush can't reach. Then we help you keep your gums healthy between visits.

Treating gum disease

  • Signs to watch for

    Gums that bleed when you brush, look red or swollen, or teeth that feel loose are worth checking.

  • Deep cleaning

    Plaque and tartar are removed from above and below the gum line so the gums can heal.

  • Ongoing care

    Regular check-ups and good daily habits keep the disease from coming back.
